Junior Researcher, M.Eng. candidate · State Key Laboratory of CNS/ATM, Beihang University

Seeking a PhD position, start September 2027

I work on UAV-assisted integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) for urban airspace. My methods are deep and multi-agent reinforcement learning for joint trajectory, beamforming and spectrum design, and my application area is air traffic management.

My M.Eng. thesis, supervised by Prof. Peng Zhao, studies joint optimization of trajectory, beamforming and power allocation for UAV-enabled ISAC. I expect to defend in May 2027. After my PhD I plan to return to Bangladesh as a university professor and build a research group.
